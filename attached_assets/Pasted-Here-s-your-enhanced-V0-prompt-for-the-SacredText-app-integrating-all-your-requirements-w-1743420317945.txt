### **V0 Prompt: "SacredText" – AI-Powered Interfaith Companion**  
*A dynamic LLM app offering virtual guides, daily blessings, cross-religious dialogue, and multilingual access—rooted in respect and modern design.*  

---

### **1. Core Features**  
#### **A. Virtual Guides (AI Avatars)**  
- **Role**: Digital representatives of each faith (e.g., a *Buddhist monk*, *Christian pastor*, *Islamic scholar*).  
- **Capabilities**:  
  - Teachings: Deliver scripture excerpts (e.g., *"Matthew 5:9"* → *"Blessed are the peacemakers..."*).  
  - Historical Context: Explain origins (e.g., *"The Five Pillars of Islam emerged in 7th-century Arabia..."*).  
  - Interpretations: Offer scholarly perspectives (e.g., *"Kabbalistic views on forgiveness"*).  
- **Design**:  
  - **Icons**: Minimalist line-art avatars (e.g., a lotus for Buddhism, cross for Christianity).  
  - **Color-Coded**: Matches faith’s palette (see table below).  

#### **B. Daily Blessings & Inspirations**  
- **Input**: User selects faith + focus (e.g., *"Gratitude"*, *"Justice"*).  
- **Output**:  
  - *"Hinduism: ‘Offer your actions to the Divine, as Krishna teaches in the Gita (3:30). Today, practice seeing work as worship.’"*  
  - Firebase stores a history for reflection.  

#### **C. Interactive Q&A Sessions**  
- **Examples**:  
  - *"How does Judaism view forgiveness?"* → Answers with Talmudic + modern rabbinic insights.  
  - *"What’s the Buddhist take on climate change?"* → Links to *engaged Buddhism* principles.  
- **Safety**: LangChain filters doctrinal conflicts unless *"Comparative Mode"* is on.  

#### **D. Interpretation Button**  
- **Feature**: After a teaching, click *"Compare Perspectives"* → Shows parallels (e.g., *Christian "love thy neighbor" vs. Buddhist "metta"*).  
- **UI**: Expandable accordion to avoid clutter.  

#### **E. Multi-Language Support**  
- **Initial Languages**: English, Spanish, Arabic, Hindi.  
- **Toggle**: Language dropdown (flags optional).  
- **Tech**: OpenAI’s translation API + LangChain to preserve doctrinal nuance.

### **3. Example User Flow**  
1. **Select Faith**: Buddhism (☸, `bg-stone-800` theme).  
2. **Daily Blessing**:  
   - *"Today: Reflect on impermanence. As the Dhammapada says, ‘All conditioned things are transient.’"*  
3. **Q&A**:  
   - User: *"How to handle anger mindfully?"*  
   - AI: *"The Buddha taught..."* → *"Compare"* button → *"Similar to Stoic ‘amor fati’ or Christian ‘turn the other cheek.’"*  
4. **Save**: Click bookmark → Stores to Firebase under *"Mindfulness"*.

### **4. Tech Stack**  
- **AI**: OpenAI (GPT-4) + LangChain for:  
  - **Doctrine Guardrails**: Prevents mixing unless comparative mode.  
  - **Translation**: Preserves nuance across languages.  
- **Backend**:  
  - Firebase Firestore: Saves blessings/Q&A history.  
  - Auth: Optional (email/anonymous).  
- **UI**: Vercel + Next.js, with:  
  - Dynamic theming (CSS variables switch palettes).  
  - Dark mode default (reduces eye strain).
